## Bug

### B1: Two quick taps on "Tiếp" skip a story box (mission-flow AC9)
- **Severity:** Medium (S3). A child who double-taps misses part of the story; the last intro box can be skipped straight into play. **Priority:** P2.
- **Build / device:** bfe3890 debug, emulator mp17863 API 36.
- **Steps:** 1. Start, which opens intro box 1. 2. Tap "Tiếp" twice, 0.15–0.2 s apart (each touch 0.05 s).
- **Expected:** Only one box advances (AC9: two taps within 0.25 s, one box).
- **Actual:** Two boxes advance, 3 of 3 times (1 → 3, 4 → 6, 6 → play, skipping box 7). A single tap advances exactly one box (3 → 4).
- **Evidence:** `tc02-intro-box1-vi.png` (box 1), `tc02-intro-after-double-tap.png` (box 3).
- **Likely cause (from reading bfe3890):** in `GameView.drainInput`, the `firedThisFrame` guard only applies to quiz cards (`key.startsWith("card")`) and only within one frame. `Game.next()` has no 0.25 s debounce.

## Observations so far

- **The UNSIGNED badge covers story text** (debug only). On the intro boxes the `UNSIGNED Cxx` badge sits on the first text line (for example, it hides "Trần, có một" on box 1). This matters if the history reviewer reads debug builds. Suggestion: draw the badge above the picture or at the line end.
- **Letterbox, judged for a child's phone (observation, for Luan or design):** the safe rect is the cutout (left 136), the status bar ignoring visibility (top 74) and the gesture zone (bottom 84). World scale k = 1.281, so the world is 1639x922 px at x 448–2088, y 74–996. Bands are 448 px on the left and 312 px on the right. **The game uses 68% of the screen width and 58% of its area.** Touch targets are fine (78–90 dp), but on a 20:9 phone the characters and story pictures are noticeably small, and the dark bands look like a broken screen to a child. Most of the loss comes from two choices: (1) the status-bar inset (74) counts even though the bars are hidden in immersive mode, and (2) the whole world is fitted inside the gesture inset, not just the pads. Fitting the world to the cutout only, and keeping the HUD and pads inside the full safe rect, would give scale 1.5 (1920 x 1080 px), **71% of the area and 80% of the width**. Filling the bands with extended scenery (sky and ground) instead of dark would also make the remaining bands look intended.
- **Debug `startScreen` extras are ignored when the app is already running** (the intent is delivered to the existing activity). QC must `force-stop` first. This only affects tooling.

- **Build swapped during the round (process):** a dev session installed a newer APK mid-round, which invalidated a full playthrough. The leader has set a rule: no dev installs on mp17863 during a QC round. QC now keeps a copy of each APK it is given.
